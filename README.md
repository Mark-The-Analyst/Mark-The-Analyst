
# Mark 

**Data Analyst — Health & Life Sciences**

I work with messy operational and biological data and turn it into things people can act on — SQL models, Python pipelines, and dashboards that answer a specific question rather than displaying everything at once.

My background is in the life sciences: a BS in Medical Biology, three years running microbiological QC in a regulated pharmaceutical lab, and an MS in Health Informatics (Bioinformatics) in progress at UP Manila. That means I'm comfortable with data that comes from instruments and protocols rather than clean exports — and with the documentation and validation habits that regulated environments demand.

Most of what I publish here sits at the intersection of health data and business analytics.

---

### What I work with

**Querying & Languages** — SQL (PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server, BigQuery), Python (pandas, NumPy), R
**Visualization** — Power BI (DAX), Tableau, Looker Studio, Excel (Power Query, PivotTables)
**Analysis** — Exploratory analysis, cohort and funnel analysis, segmentation, A/B testing, forecasting, anomaly detection
**Pipelines** — ETL, data modeling, star schema, cleaning and validation workflows
**Domain** — Health informatics, bioinformatics, GLP/cGMP, statistical process control
